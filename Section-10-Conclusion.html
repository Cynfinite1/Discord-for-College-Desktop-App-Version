10. Conclusion

Congratulations, you've reached the end of this tutorial, and you are ready to use Discord with your class or group.

You can now create an account, join your group's server, find the right channels, send messages and files, control your notifications and privacy, join voice meetings, share your screen, and fix common problems on your own.

More information in this tutorial

Discord Help Center

For anything this tutorial does not cover, visit the Discord Help Center. It is Discord's official support site, and you can search it for any feature, setting, or error message.

  • Discord Help Center: The official home of every Discord help article. Search it for any feature or error message.
  • Beginner's Guide to Discord: An overview of accounts, servers, text and voice channels, screen sharing, and profiles.

  • Go Live and Screen Share: How to share one window or your entire screen in a voice channel.
  • Discord Voice and Video Troubleshooting Guide: More fixes for microphone, speaker, and camera problems.
  • Four Steps to a Safer Account: Discord's Safety Center guide to passwords, privacy settings, and blocking.
