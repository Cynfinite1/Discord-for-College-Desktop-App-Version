9.1 Audio and Video Problems

Most audio and video problems come from a muted button, the wrong device, or a Windows permission. Find your problem below and follow its fixes in order.

Others Cannot Hear Me

Work through these steps in order. Stop as soon as your group can hear you.

  1. If the microphone button in the user panel has a red line through it, click it to unmute yourself.
  2. If the headphone button in the user panel has a red line through it, click it to undeafen yourself.
  3. If a red microphone icon appears next to your name under the voice channel, ask a moderator to unmute you. This icon means a moderator muted you for the whole server, and you cannot unmute yourself.
  4. Click the gear icon (User Settings) in the user panel.
  5. Click Voice & Video in the list on the left.
  6. Click the Voice tab.
  7. Open the Input Device menu.
  8. Click the microphone you are using.
  9. If the Input Volume slider is low, drag it to the right.
  10. If Input Mode is set to Push to Talk, click Voice Activity. With Push to Talk, Discord only sends your voice while you hold down a key.
  11. Under Mic Test, click Let's Check.
  12. Speak into your microphone. If the bar moves and you hear yourself, your microphone works.
  13. Click Stop Testing.

If the bar did not move, Windows may be blocking Discord from using your microphone:

  1. Press Windows + I to open Windows Settings.
  2. Click Privacy & security.
  3. Click Microphone.
  4. Turn on Let desktop apps access your microphone.

Result: Your group can hear you, and a green ring appears around your picture when you talk.

NoteIf you run the Mic Test while you are in a voice channel, Discord mutes and deafens you until you click Stop Testing.

I Cannot Hear Others

  1. If the headphone button in the user panel has a red line through it, click it.
  2. Right-click a teammate's name under the voice channel.
  3. If the User Volume slider is all the way to the left, drag it to the right.
  4. If Mute is checked, click it to uncheck it.
  5. Click the gear icon (User Settings) in the user panel.
  6. Click Voice & Video in the list on the left.
  7. Open the Output Device menu.
  8. Click the speakers or headphones you are using.
  9. If the Output Volume slider is low, drag it to the right.
  10. Click the speaker icon at the bottom-right of the Windows taskbar.
  11. If the volume is low or muted, drag the volume slider to the right.

Result: You can hear your teammates in the voice channel.

My Camera Will Not Turn On

  1. Close other apps that may be using your camera, such as Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or the Windows Camera app.
  2. Click the gear icon (User Settings) in the user panel.
  3. Click Voice & Video in the list on the left.
  4. Click the Video tab.
  5. Open the Camera menu.
  6. Click the camera you are using.
  7. Click Test Video to check that your picture appears.

If no picture appears, Windows may be blocking Discord from using your camera:

  1. Press Windows + I to open Windows Settings.
  2. Click Privacy & security.
  3. Click Camera.
  4. Turn on Camera access.
  5. Turn on Let desktop apps access your camera.

If the camera still does not work, restart Discord:

  1. Right-click the Discord icon in the Windows notification area at the bottom-right of the taskbar.
  2. Click Quit Discord.
  3. Open Discord again from the Start menu or your desktop.

Result: Your camera preview appears, and you can turn on your camera in the voice channel.

CautionClicking the X at the top-right of the Discord window does not fully close the app. Discord keeps running in the notification area, so use Quit Discord when a restart is needed.
