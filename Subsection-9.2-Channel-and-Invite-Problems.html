9.2 Channel and Invite Problems

Missing channels and broken invite links usually have a simple cause. Find your problem below and follow its fixes in order.

You Cannot See a Channel

  1. If a category name in the channel list has an arrow pointing right, click the name to show the channels under it.
  2. Click the server name at the top of the channel list.
  3. If Hide Muted Channels is checked, click it to show muted channels again.
  4. Right-click the grayed-out channel.
  5. Click Unmute Channel.

Result: The channel appears in your channel list.

NoteIf the channel is still missing, ask a moderator for help. You may need a role that can see the channel. For example: "Hi, I am in Group 3. Could you give me the @Group 3 role so I can see our channel?"

An Invite Link Does Not Work

  1. Check the name in the user panel to make sure you are logged in to the right Discord account.
  2. In Discord, click the plus button (Add a Server) in the server list.
  3. Click Join a Server.
  4. Copy the whole invite link again, and paste it into the Invite Link box. It should start with discord.gg/ and have no spaces before or after it.
  5. Click Join Server.
  6. If Discord says the invite is invalid or expired, ask your group for a new link that is set to never expire.

Result: The server opens, and its icon appears in your server list.

NoteA free Discord account can belong to up to 100 servers. If you reached that limit, right-click a server you no longer use, click Leave Server, and then try the invite again.
