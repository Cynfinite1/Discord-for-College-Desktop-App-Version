7.2 Sharing Your Screen

Sharing your screen lets your group see your slides, documents, or code during a call. This page shows you how to start and stop a share.

Choosing a Window or Entire Screen

You can share your screen in any voice channel where your server allows it. Sharing one window is usually safer, because teammates see only that window and not your notifications or other open apps.

  1. Join a voice channel. Subsection 7.1 shows how.
  2. Close any windows or browser tabs that show private information, such as your email or bank account.
  3. Click the Share Your Screen button (a monitor with an arrow) in the voice status panel.
  4. Click the Applications tab to share one window, or the Entire Screen tab to share everything on your monitor.
  5. Click the window or screen you want to share.

Result: A small preview of your share appears, and a red LIVE label appears next to your name in the voice channel. Teammates click your name to watch.

NoteLeave the quality settings as they are unless your group asks you to change them. To share a video with sound, such as a clip in your slides, turn on the sound option before you start sharing.

Stopping a Share

  1. Click the Stop Streaming button (a monitor with an X) in the voice status panel.

Result: Your share ends, and the LIVE label disappears from your name. You stay connected to the voice channel, so you can keep talking.

NoteClicking Disconnect also stops your share, but it removes you from the call.
